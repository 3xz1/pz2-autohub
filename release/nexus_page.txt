=== TITLE ===
AutoHub - Stop Inbreeding (Auto Sell, Release or Storage)

=== BRIEF OVERVIEW (Nexus "summary", max 350 characters) ===
Stops inbreeding before it starts. AutoHub finds adults living with a parent or an opposite-sex sibling and moves them out for you: to Animal Storage, by Quick Trade or by Quick Release. It can also put the young on contraception. Separate settings for habitats, aquariums and vivariums. Off by default.

=== DESCRIPTION (paste into the BBCode editor) ===
[size=5][b]AutoHub[/b][/size]
[i]Finds related animals living together and moves them out, so your habitats don't inbreed.[/i]

Babies grow up, and before you notice, a daughter is breeding with her father. AutoHub checks your habitats, aquariums and vivariums for related adults and moves them out for you, with the game's own actions. It can also put newborns on contraception while they live with their family. Open it from the game's Esc menu or the small AutoHub button.

[size=4][b]Who leaves[/b][/size]
AutoHub uses the game's own rule for related animals: a parent and its child, or two animals with at least one parent in common. In one habitat, aquarium or vivarium:
[list]
[*]An [b]adult living with its mother or father[/b] leaves.
[*]An [b]adult brother and sister[/b] (half-siblings too) both leave. This rule can be switched off.
[*][b]Fish shoals[/b]: when a tank has more shoals of one species than you allow ([b]Shoals per species[/b], 1 to 10 or No limit, default 1), the oldest ones leave and the youngest stay. This catches a shoal that split in two. Other species never count, so a tank with Clownfish and Tangs is fine. Set it higher, or to No limit, to keep extra shoals you bought.
[/list]
Same-sex siblings stay together. Juveniles and elderly animals never leave. Cousins don't count, as in the game.

[b]Who leaves: Grown up here[/b] (per kind): only animals AutoHub saw growing up in that habitat leave once they are adults. Adults you place there yourself stay, even next to their brothers, sisters or parents, and get no contraception, so they can breed. Their young that grow up there leave as usual.

[size=4][b]When it checks[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Off[/b] (default): only when you press [b]Check all[/b] or [b]Check the open habitat[/b].
[*][b]New adults[/b]: animals that grew up in their habitat leave once they are adults. AutoHub keeps checking them until they are gone, so a full storage or a park reload doesn't make it forget them. When a park opens (or you switch to another zoo), it checks everyone once.
[*][b]Always[/b]: every animal at every check, however long it has lived there.
[/list]
Auto checks every 5 seconds of real time by default (1 s to 5 min), whatever the game speed.

[size=4][b]Where they go[/b][/size]
You choose, separately for habitats, aquariums and vivariums:
[list]
[*][b]Animal Storage[/b] (default): a keeper carries them to your Trade Centre. You can bring them back later.
[*][b]Quick Trade[/b]: sold at once, like the game's Quick Trade button.
[*][b]Quick Release[/b]: released into the wild at once, like the game's Quick Release.
[/list]
[b]Keep good genes[/b]: with Quick Trade / Release you can set a minimum Vitality, Longevity, Fertility and Immunity. Animals that have to leave and reach every minimum go to Animal Storage for your next breeding round; the others are sold or released. Per sex you can choose "Always sell", e.g. sell every male and keep only the best females.

If the game doesn't allow a Quick Trade or Release for an animal (the same animals whose buttons are greyed out in the game), it goes to Animal Storage instead and the log says why. Animals the game says are [b]worth 0[/b] go to Animal Storage too, unless you set "Worth 0" to "Anyway".

Every trade and release is logged with its amount, and [b]Earnings in this park[/b] adds them up per habitats, aquariums, vivariums and all.

[size=4][b]Auto contraception[/b][/size]
Newborns living with their mother or father of the other sex (or a brother or sister, with that rule on) get contraception right away, with the same toggle as the animal's info panel.
[list]
[*][b]On its own[/b] (Auto off): animals stay where they are, they just can't breed with their family.
[*][b]With Auto[/b]: the young are on contraception while they grow up and leave once they are adults.
[*]Once such an animal lives without those relatives, AutoHub takes the contraception off again (only for animals it put on contraception itself). If you switch it off by hand, AutoHub leaves it off.
[/list]

[size=4][b]The panel[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Overview[/b]: what each kind is set to, when the last check ran, [b]Check all[/b] and [b]Check the open habitat[/b] (two clicks, so nothing moves by accident), the earnings and the last animals moved out.
[*][b]Settings[/b]: per kind (habitats, aquariums, vivariums): on/off, Auto (Off / New adults / Always), who leaves (all related / grown up here), action, Worth 0, keep good genes (with gene minimums and per-sex rules), shoals per species (aquariums and vivariums), brother + sister, contraception; one click copies them to the other two. General: how often Auto checks (1 s to 5 min, default 5 s), size, screen button, and short help texts if you want them.
[*][b]Log[/b]: everything AutoHub did, contraception changes, and [b]Diagnose[/b] for bug reports.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Good to know[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Everything is off by default. Nothing happens until you switch Auto or contraception on, or press a Check button.
[*]For Animal Storage your zoo needs a Trade Centre with free storage space, as for the normal game action.
[*]Settings are saved in your player profile, for all parks; the earnings are saved per park.
[*]Quick Trade / Release values: if the game hasn't loaded an animal's value yet, AutoHub waits up to 15 seconds for it.
[*]Vivariums: the game loads family data in the background. The first check after loading a park may say "family data is still loading"; check again a few seconds later.
[*]Light on performance: each animal is read once per check, and its sex and parents are remembered while it is in the park. Diagnose shows how many game calls the last check made.
[*]Franchise: Quick Trade and Quick Release are not tested there yet. If the game refuses them, the animals go to Animal Storage instead.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Installation[/b][/size]
[list=1]
[*]In Steam: right-click Planet Zoo 2 > Manage > Browse local files.
[*]Copy the [b]Win64[/b] folder from the zip into that game folder and choose "Replace" / merge. Nothing of the game is overwritten; only a new PZ2AutoHub folder is added.
[*]Check that [i]Planet Zoo 2\Win64\ovldata\PZ2AutoHub\Main.ovl[/i] exists.
[*]Start the game. The one-time "modifications detected" notice is normal.
[/list]
[b]Update:[/b] copy the new Win64 folder over the old one. Your settings carry over.
[b]Uninstall:[/b] delete [i]Planet Zoo 2\Win64\ovldata\PZ2AutoHub[/i].

[size=4][b]Compatibility[/b][/size]
Works alongside [b]Guardian[/b] (they share the in-park screen and the "Mods" box in the Esc menu) [b]Mike's QoL Toolbox[/b], [b]PZ2 Essentials[/b] and [b]More Path Stamp Sizes[/b] (any install order). No game files are changed. The only thing written is AutoHub's own small entry in your player profile; nothing goes into park saves or online saves.
